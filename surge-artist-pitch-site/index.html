At nimmersatt, we believe
in a simple truth:

you are what you eat.

Ideas we consume shape the work we create. That's why we begin every project by understanding culture before pushing content. Based on research, insights and generational decoding, we identify what resonates organically, how communities of the next generation interact, we are able to embed brands from within through authentic participation.

We operate as a creative studio, artist management and production company in one. Rather than fitting projects into fixed agency structures, we curate bespoke creative units from our roster of directors, designers, photographers, strategists, editors, developers and producers. Whether a project requires a single specialist or an end-to-end team, every unit is assembled around the brief, not the other way around.

Our network brings together established industry professionals and the next generation of creative voices. By pairing senior expertise with emerging talent, we create work that combines experience with stylizing perspectives while remaining adaptable to all scales and budgets.

What we make mirrors what we consume. We've built a collective of Berlin's most exceptional visual minds, found on its streets and in its digital networks.

Our artists are self-made: they skipped the academic route for raw experimentation, and reject outdated workflows.

We don't just manage talent, we run the whole process, from first input to a polished, cinematic output, the link between established brands and the new generation.

Let’s address the elephant in the room. Where is the fucking catch?

Non-Exclusivity

You are not signed away. Your own clients stay your own clients, and nothing stops you from working outside the network. What we do ask is that everything touching our name, our references and our contacts runs through us for as long as we work together and for a year after. We represent you worldwide, we speak to clients with one voice, and we price your work so nobody undercuts the room you are standing in.

Commission

Twenty percent of the net fee, on projects we bring in. Nothing hidden behind it. Your day rate is set together and neither side moves it alone, so no discount happens over your head. You get paid within thirty days of the client paying us, and if a job goes wrong, the crew around you gets paid before anyone argues about the rest.

Exit

Open-ended, three months on probation, then one month’s notice to the end of the month. No lock-in, no ten-page exit. Everything already running gets finished and settled properly, whoever ends it and for whatever reason. The only thing that outlives the contract is a twelve-month agreement not to walk away with the clients we introduced you to.

01

Cultural Craft & Quality Standard

Decode Before Creating: Immerse yourself in the brief and culture—no empty noise, only work that resonates authentically with modern communities.

Cinematic Standard: Whether working across strategy, set, design, or post-production, maintain a high-end, cinematic standard across all budgets.

02

Collaborative Ownership & Respect

Cross-Generational Mindset: Combine established senior expertise with emerging perspectives through mutual respect and openness.

Lean Execution: Work directly without agency bloat—take proactive ownership of your role and add tangible value to every deliverable.

03

Transparent Commitment & Mutual Growth

Clear & Early Communication: Speak up early about capacity, boundaries, or bottlenecks before small issues turn into production problems.

Building a Career, Not Just a Calendar: Align with clarified briefs and deadlines, using every job to elevate your portfolio and the collective work.
